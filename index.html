Traffic Analytics

Australian Traffic Enforcement Intelligence

Welcome to the central intelligence hub. This dashboard presents the official Annual Enforcement series, allowing you to explore interactive traffic violation data and monitor enforcement outcomes. The current dataset tracks:

  • Mobile phone fines issued, including camera detection.
  • Speeding fines issued, including camera detection.
  • Seatbelt fines issued by police and camera detection.
  • Unlicensed driving issued by police (introduced from 2023).
Methodology Note
  • Arrests and charges have been collected from data reported since 2023, but may not be applicable for all states and territories.
  • Data is subject to revision.
